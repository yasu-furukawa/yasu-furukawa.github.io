The Hue Hexagon

Stand the RGB cube on its black corner and look straight down the gray diagonal. The cube's outline collapses into a hexagon with six colored corners, 60° apart. That is where hue's 360° comes from. Drag the tilt to watch it happen.

The two hue numbers agree at the six corners and drift by up to 1.2° in between.

Stand it on black

The cube rests on the black corner, white directly above it. The dashed line through them is the gray axis, length √3. Value measures height along it.

Six corners, two heights

Red, green and blue sit below the midplane. Yellow, cyan and magenta sit the same distance above it. The six corners form a crown, not a flat ring.

Look down the axis

Height disappears and the crown becomes a regular hexagon. Hue is the angle of your color around it, measured from red. Gray lands dead center, where no angle exists.
