Convolution vs. Correlation

One sign separates the two sums. Convolution pairs indices that add to n, which makes it symmetric in f and g. Correlation pairs indices that differ by n, which does not.

Convolution

(f * g)[n] = Σk f[k] g[n − k]

The sum runs over every pair with k + (n−k) = n. Swap f and g and the pairing stays the same.

Cross-correlation

(f ⋆ g)[n] = Σk f[k] g[n + k]

The sum runs over every pair with j − k = n. Swap f and g and n flips sign, which breaks the symmetry.
