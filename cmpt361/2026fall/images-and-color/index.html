LAB Color Space (CIELAB)
  • Also written L*a*b* — defined by the International Commission on Illumination (CIE), 1976.
  • L* = perceptual lightness (0 = black, 100 = white).  a* = green ↔ red.  b* = blue ↔ yellow.
  • Device-independent — defined relative to the CIE standard observer, not tied to any monitor, printer, or sensor.
  • Perceptually uniform — equal numerical ΔE changes ≈ equal perceived colour differences. Used in industry for colour QC.
  • Widely used for colour segmentation, colour transfer, and image-difference metrics.
  • MATLAB: rgb2lab / lab2rgb  (Image Processing Toolbox)

Summary & Exercises

PNG vs. JPG

PNG — lossless, exact pixel values.  JPG — lossy, quality/size trade-off.
MATLAB: imwrite(A, 'out.jpg', 'quality', 75)  (range 0–100; lower = more compression).
Bit depth: 8-bit uint8, 16-bit uint16, 32-bit float (.exr, .dng for HDR / raw).

MATLAB Exercise

Load color.jpg, convert with im2double, separate and display R / G / B channels.
Export at different quality levels: compare imwrite(A,'out.jpg','quality',16) vs 90.
Also explore .exr (HDR) and .dng (raw camera) formats.

Photo Recolouring

Work in HSV or LAB space to manipulate colour independently of brightness.
Shift the Hue channel, or adjust the a* / b* axes in LAB, to recolour objects selectively.
Historical example: Prokudin-Gorsky's three-exposure RGB process (1909–1915).
