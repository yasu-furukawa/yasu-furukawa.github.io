Demo 2 · width, then depth again

Many kernels make many channels — and the next kernels get deeper

One kernel gives one output map. Use five different 3×3×3 kernels on the same RGB image and you get five maps — stack them and you have a feature image with five channels. The next layer's kernels must therefore be 3×3×5. Seven of them give a seven-channel image, and the pattern repeats: number of kernels in = number of channels out.
