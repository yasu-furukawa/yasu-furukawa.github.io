Radial distortion

A real lens does not put a point where the pinhole model says it should. It leaves the direction alone and changes only the distance from the centre of the image — pulling points in, or pushing them out, by an amount that grows with that distance.

The idea

1
Strip out the intrinsics, so the principal point is the origin
x = (u − cx) / fx ,   y = (v − cy) / fy
2
Measure how far out the point is
r = √ x2 + y2
3
Work out the stretch for that distance — even powers only, because it cannot depend on direction
L(r) = 1 + k1r2 + k2r4 + k3r6
4
Slide the point along its own ray by that factor — this is the whole model
(xd, yd) = L(r) · (x, y)    so    rd = L(r) · r
5
Put the intrinsics back to get the pixel the sensor records
ud = fxxd + cx ,   vd = fyyd + cy

Reading the result

Correction is a backward warp. For every pixel of the corrected image we already know its ideal coordinate, so we push it forward through L(r) and sample the recorded image there. The forward model is a closed-form polynomial, so nothing has to be inverted — which is why correction runs as a fixed lookup table (initUndistortRectifyMap) built once per calibration. Clicking in the recorded frame does need the inverse, and because the direction is untouched that is only a one-dimensional root find: solve rL(r) = rd for r.

Barrel distortion costs resolution at the edges. Every corner pixel of the corrected image samples a point the lens had squeezed inward, so it is still on the sensor and the corrected frame fills. But it is reconstructed from a small, densely packed patch of pixels, which is why corrected corners look soft. Push k1 positive for the opposite case: pincushion sends points outward, past the sensor edge, and the corrected image comes back with hatched corners nothing was recorded for.

Watch where the target's edge sits. Under a strong barrel the checkerboard pulls well in from the frame, leaving sensor area carrying nothing but blank card. A calibration capture should push the target right out to the corners — large r is the only place where k2 and k3 are distinguishable at all.
