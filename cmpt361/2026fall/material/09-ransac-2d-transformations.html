RANSAC for 2D Transformations

A picture of a house and a dog is carried into a second image by an unknown 2D transformation. Arrows link the putative correspondences, and some of them are wrong. RANSAC repeatedly draws the minimal number of correspondences that determine the transformation, fits it exactly, and counts how many of the remaining correspondences agree.
