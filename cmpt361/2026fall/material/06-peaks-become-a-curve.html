Each sine curve in the signal contributes one number, and those numbers sit in order along the frequency axis. With two ingredients there are two of them; with twenty-four the list is no longer something you read off one by one — it is a curve. The frequency domain is a 1D function of u, exactly as the signal is a 1D function of x.

Window one unit wide, u = whole cycles across it. The area is exactly half the weight — hence the × 2. Drag the middle plot to move the marker; Play sweeps the area.
