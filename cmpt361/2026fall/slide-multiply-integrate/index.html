Every t you try adds one point here — the result of sliding, multiplying and integrating is itself a function of t.

(f * g)(t) = ∫ f(τ) g(t − τ) dτ    (f ⋆ g)(t) = ∫ f(τ) g(t + τ) dτ
t plays the part of n, τ the part of k  —  g(t−τ) is the continuous g[n−k]
